# BIOSDV

> Biology, Chemistry, Research & Biotechnology.

## 🧬 About BIOSDV

BIOSDV is a personal scientific ecosystem focused on biology, chemistry, biotechnology, and scientific research.

The project was created to collect and document scientific work, research projects, experiments, observations, and results related to the chemical and biological sciences.

BIOSDV is continuously developing through new research, experiments, and scientific projects.

## 🔬 Research

Research is the main direction of BIOSDV.

The ecosystem includes:

* Chemical research
* Biological research
* Biotechnology
* Scientific experiments
* Analysis and documentation of results

Each project is documented separately, with its purpose, methodology, observations, results, and conclusions.

## 🧪 Projects

### Aspirin

Aspirin is a research project dedicated to the study of aspirin and its chemical and biological properties.

The project contains the research process, collected information, analysis, and conclusions.

🔗 [Research Work 1. Aspirin.](https://github.com/Biosdv-biotech/Aspirin)

## 🧫 Experiments

Experimental work is an important part of BIOSDV.

Experiments are used to study chemical and biological processes in practice, observe their results, and compare them with theoretical knowledge.

All relevant observations and results are documented as part of the ecosystem.
